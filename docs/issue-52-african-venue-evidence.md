# Issue 52: African venues, research round 4

Reviewed 2026-10-22. Research only: no registry/repository edits, GitHub writes, commits, or delegated agents. Read docs/issue-52-venue-audit.md and all five requested registry records.

## Recommendation and the actual problem

All five identities can be treated as resolved for a selector that accepts documented historical names. Retain their existing IDs and display labels. None needs a speculative merge or modern stadium name. The previous unresolved flags conflated three separate questions: (1) which physical cricket site hosted the historical matches; (2) what name a primary source actually gives it; (3) whether a 2026 source certifies a current commercial naming agreement. The first two have evidence; the third should be a qualified provenance limit, not an unresolved physical identity.

There is now particularly strong operator evidence for Affies that the prior search missed. United and Wanderers have explicit 2023–24 board ground/fixture naming and a board report listing them alongside the new FNB ground as three venues. Jaffery has original participant directions, ICC history, and 2025 first-hand community tournament reporting. Mombasa has primary ICC ground/fixture naming and club-owned identity/address corroboration, although its operator homepage actually returned an empty response and cannot certify a separate modern oval name.

Do not call all five '2026 sponsorship-certified'. Suggested conceptual scopes below are descriptive; use the existing registry's supported vocabulary or deliberately add appropriate documented scopes. They are not requests to edit code.

### odi-ground-003 — Affies Park, Windhoek

Recommended label: retain **Affies Park, Windhoek**. Optional search aliases Affiespark and WAP cricket ground are supported; do not attach FNB or Wanderers as aliases. Recommended scope: **operator_facility_name_and_historical_board_ground**; verified identity, operator facility nomenclature retrieved today, historical individual cricket-ground name.

Proof chain: S1 exact PNG–USA match at Affies + S2 exact forthcoming same match at WAP -> WAP operator S3 explicitly names Affiespark and its cricket field -> S4 same-site history and S5 2026 facility-name usage. No rename evidence found. A ground may retain a historical display name even when no 2026 ODI is scheduled there. Current school sport use is not proof the cricket field vanished; S3 affirmatively lists it. Affies is a cricket field within a sports facility, not the whole Wanderers cricket oval. The school's Eros teaching campus is separate from its Affiespark sports grounds.

Prior flag: justified only as a statement that an exact current commercial cricket-oval certificate had not been found; not justified as unresolved identity/name. New operator page resolves even the weaker current facility-name question. No active international-use guarantee or sponsorship certification should be asserted.
